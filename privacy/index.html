Privacy

This site counts visits so that the town and its businesses can see whether it is useful. It does so without cookies and without keeping anything that identifies you.

Preview

This is a preview of the site. On this preview nothing is counted or stored: no page views, clicks or trip planner searches. The counting described below starts when the site goes live.

What is recorded

  • Page views. The page, the date and time, and the website you came from, if any.
  • Clicks on listings. When you follow a link to a business website, tap a phone number or ask for directions, the site counts one click for that business.
  • Trip planner searches. The words you type into the trip planner are stored so that we can see what visitors look for. Please do not type personal details into it.

What is not recorded

  • No cookies are set for visitors and no advertising or social media trackers are loaded.
  • Your IP address is not stored. To tell one visitor from another on the same day, the site makes a one-way code from the address and browser type. The code changes every day and cannot be turned back into an address.

The trip planner and AI

When AI matching is switched on, the text of your search is sent to an AI service together with the public list of businesses, so that it can match your request to listings. Nothing else about you is sent.

Fonts

The typefaces on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch them.

Listings you send us

If you send a business listing, the business details are published once approved. Your own name and contact details are seen only by the site team.
