Things to do in Vaal Marina

Most people come for the water. There is enough on land to fill the rest of the weekend.

On the water

The dam is the reason the town exists, and there is a great deal of it.

With more than 800 kilometres of shoreline, the Vaal Dam has room for power boats, water-skiers, jet skis, kayaks and yachts without anyone getting in anyone else's way. Vaal Marina sits on the Gauteng bank, a short trip from Johannesburg and the East Rand.

Visitors can launch at resorts with slipways, and property owners can join the communal waterfront for an annual fee. The water level changes through the year. When the dam is low, rocks that are normally well under water come close to the surface, so ask locally before you open the throttle.

Sailing

Inland sailing at about 1,500 metres, with a club that welcomes visitors.

Highveld wind and open water have made the Vaal Dam one of the centres of inland sailing in South Africa. Pennant Nine Yacht Club, on Sailfish Street, sails keelboats and runs a junior section in Optimists and Dabchicks. It has a slipway, a clubhouse and courtesy moorings for visiting boats.

The big date on the dam is the Round the Island Race, a mass-start race around the island that holds the Guinness World Record for the largest inland yacht race.

Fishing

Carp and barbel from the bank, the jetty or a boat.

The dam is well stocked with carp and barbel, and people fish it all year from the bank and from boats. When the water is low, long stretches of shore turn into sandy beaches where families set up a gazebo for the day.

Several places to stay have their own waterfront, so you can fish a few metres from where you are braaiing.

Golf

A golf course beside a marina.

Harbour Town, on Ring Road, describes itself as the only gated marina and golf estate on the Gauteng side of the dam. Alongside the course it has tennis courts, walking trails and its own slipways.

Running, cycling and birds

Quiet roads, big skies and a great many birds.

There is a parkrun every Saturday morning, and the roads around town are quiet enough for running and cycling the rest of the week.

The dam is one of the best inland birding sites in the province. Each January BirdLife Vaaldam organises the Vaaldam Big Bird Count; one count recorded 83 species and 97,500 birds. At night you may hear eagle owls and jackals.

Hot-air balloon flights operate in the wider Vaal area when the weather allows, and photographers come for the sunsets across the water.

Day trips

Three outings within easy reach.

Suikerbosrand Nature Reserve lies to the north, near Heidelberg. It protects rocky Highveld grassland between 1,545 and 1,917 metres, with hiking and cycling trails and about 200 bird species.

Deneysville, on the Free State bank beside the dam wall, is the oldest of the dam villages. Its stone church on the waterfront began as a hunting lodge.

Further west, the Vredefort Dome near Parys is the eroded heart of the largest known meteorite impact structure on Earth, and a World Heritage Site.

Getting here

Vaal Marina is about an hour's drive from Alberton and the south of Johannesburg, and about 15 minutes more from the East Rand or West Rand.

The town is just off the R54, the road between Vereeniging and Villiers. The turn-off is roughly 15 kilometres from the junction where the Heidelberg and Deneysville roads meet. Signs from Johannesburg mark the route as the Vaal Marina Meander.

Common questions

Where is Vaal Marina?

Vaal Marina is on the Gauteng shore of the Vaal Dam, in the Midvaal Local Municipality. It lies just off the R54 between Vereeniging and Villiers.

How far is Vaal Marina from Johannesburg?

It is about an hour's drive from Alberton and Johannesburg South, and roughly 15 minutes more from the East Rand or West Rand.

What is there to do in Vaal Marina?

Most visitors come for the water: power boating, water-skiing, sailing, kayaking and fishing. On land there is a Saturday parkrun, golf at Harbour Town, cycling, bird watching and day trips to Suikerbosrand Nature Reserve.

Can I launch my boat at Vaal Marina?

Yes. Some resorts have slipways for guests and day visitors, the yacht club has a slipway and visitor moorings, and property owners can join the communal waterfront. Check the Boating and fishing section of the directory for current options.

Is there accommodation in Vaal Marina?

Yes. There are self-catering resorts, guest houses and private holiday homes, many of them on or near the water. The Places to stay section of the directory lists them, and the trip planner can match a group size to places that fit.

What fish are in the Vaal Dam?

The dam is known for carp and barbel (sharptooth catfish). People fish from the bank, from jetties and from boats throughout the year.

How big is the Vaal Dam?

The Vaal Dam covers about 320 square kilometres and has more than 800 kilometres of shoreline. It holds about 2.6 billion cubic metres of water and borders Gauteng, the Free State and Mpumalanga.

How old is Vaal Marina?

The dam was completed in 1938. The town came much later: accounts put its beginnings in the 1970s, and it was proclaimed a holiday township in the mid-1980s.
