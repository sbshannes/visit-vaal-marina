Harbour Town

Gated marina and golf estate on the Gauteng side of the Vaal Dam

A secure residential marina estate about an hour from Johannesburg. It describes itself as the only gated security residential marina and golf estate on the Gauteng side of the Vaal Dam.

The estate has a golf course, internal dams, parks and play areas, a dual slipway, a clubhouse, tennis courts and walking trails.
