Pennant Nine Yacht Club

Yacht club with keelboat sailing, a junior section and moorings for visiting boats

A yacht club on the Vaal Dam that sails keelboat classes and runs a junior section for Optimist and Dabchick sailors.

The clubhouse has a bar, a television lounge and a kitchen for members and visitors. There is a slipway and gantry, six courtesy moorings for visiting boats, a small pool and playground equipment. Camping and caravans are welcome by prior arrangement.
