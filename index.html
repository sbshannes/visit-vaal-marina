Vaal Marina

A small town on the Gauteng shore of the Vaal Dam, about an hour from Johannesburg. Come for the water and stay for the weekend.

Write it the way you would say it. The trip planner matches each thing on your list to local businesses.

An hour south of Johannesburg the road ends at the water. Vaal Marina sits on the Gauteng shore of a dam so large that its shoreline runs for more than 800 kilometres and touches three provinces.

What people come for

Boating and water sports

More than 800 km of shoreline for power boats, skiers, jet skis and paddlers.

Sailing

Keelboats and dinghies on Highveld wind, and a yacht club with visitor moorings.

Fishing

Carp and barbel from the bank, a jetty or a boat, all year round.

A weekend on the water

Chalets, guest houses and holiday homes, many with their own waterfront.

Golf

A course beside the marina at Harbour Town.

Slow mornings

A Saturday parkrun, quiet roads for cycling, and birds by the thousand.

A drift, a dam and a town

The town is young, but the ground it stands on has a long memory: a river crossing where a British officer was shot in 1880, a dam built by 800 men in the Depression, and airliners that landed on the water.

  1. 1880 The night at Lies Drift A British officer is shot while crossing the Vaal, and a farmer goes into exile for decades.
  2. 1933 to 1938 A dam built in the Depression About 800 men, two shillings a day, and a wall that first overflowed in December 1938.
  3. 1948 Flying boats on the Highveld For a short time the airliner from England landed on the dam.
  4. 1970s and 1980s A town for people who love the water Plots are laid out on the Gauteng shore, on streets named after fish.
  5. 1996 The flood of 1996 The largest flood ever recorded at the dam fills it to 118.5 percent.
